ERA5 is ECMWF's fifth-generation atmospheric reanalysis.

ERA5 model-level parameters are archived in GRIB2 format.

ERA5 pressure-level data can be retrieved from the Copernicus Climate Data Store.

The CDS API allows Python programs to request ERA5 datasets programmatically.

ecCodes is ECMWF software for decoding and encoding GRIB and BUFR messages.
